# Mythology

Socarengue is a serialized mythology universe about inherited rhythm, emotional memory, music as power, city memory, symbolic objects, hidden resonance, and legacy.

The world should feel grounded but spiritually charged: street-level, musical, humid, haunted by memory, and alive with frequencies most people have forgotten how to hear.

## Core Premise

Socarengue treats emotion as something that can linger, transmit, distort, and be inherited.

Songs are not just songs. Objects are not just objects. Streets are not just streets. A chorus, a drum pattern, a photograph, a necklace, a cracked speaker, a dance floor, or a city corner can hold emotional residue.

Some people can feel these residues more clearly than others. Some can shape them. Some are shaped by them.

## Mythic Themes

- **Inherited rhythm:** families, neighborhoods, and communities pass down patterns before anyone names them.
- **Emotional memory:** strong feelings can attach to places, music, objects, and bodies.
- **Music as power:** rhythm can reveal, protect, summon, fracture, conceal, or heal.
- **Hidden resonance:** the world contains frequencies beneath visible events.
- **Legacy:** characters live inside unfinished emotional histories.
- **Symbolic objects:** relics act as containers for memory, conflict, and revelation.
- **City memory:** the city remembers what institutions, families, and lovers try to bury.

## Archive Logic

The audience should encounter the mythology through recovered materials:

- comic issues
- soundtrack fragments
- field notes
- character dossiers
- symbolic artifacts
- alternate pages
- recovered images
- annotated lyrics
- city records
- visual experiments

The platform should reveal mythology in layers. The archive can clarify relationships, but it should not flatten the story into exposition.

## Knowledge States

World material should be able to exist in different knowledge states:

- **Known:** clear to characters and audience.
- **Suspected:** hinted through artifacts, symbols, or repeated patterns.
- **Hidden:** known to creators but not yet public.
- **Contradicted:** competing accounts exist.
- **Mythologized:** emotionally true even if historically unstable.

These states matter because Socarengue is not only about what happened. It is about how memory carries, mutates, and sings.

## Tone Rules

The mythology should feel:

- intimate before epic
- musical before technical
- symbolic before explanatory
- mysterious without becoming vague
- emotionally direct without becoming sentimental
- grounded even when spiritually charged

Avoid turning resonance into a hard magic chart too early. Rules should create continuity, not drain the feeling from the world.
