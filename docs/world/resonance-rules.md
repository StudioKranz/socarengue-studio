# Resonance Rules

Resonance is the hidden emotional frequency system of Socarengue.

It is not just magic. It is memory, rhythm, feeling, place, body, object, and sound interacting under pressure.

## Core Definition

Resonance is the way emotional memory persists and transmits through music, objects, places, images, and people.

When a feeling is strong enough, repeated enough, or inherited deeply enough, it can leave a trace. That trace can be heard, felt, awakened, distorted, or carried.

## Core Rules

### 1. Emotion Leaves Traces

Powerful emotional events can imprint on:

- songs
- instruments
- recordings
- streets
- rooms
- clothing
- jewelry
- photographs
- handwritten notes
- comic pages
- symbols

### 2. Rhythm Can Unlock Memory

Certain rhythms can reveal buried emotional information.

This may appear as:

- flashes of memory
- bodily recognition
- sound distortion
- visual symbolism
- recovered fragments
- emotional overwhelm
- sudden clarity

### 3. Objects Can Hold Charge

Symbolic objects can carry resonance because of what happened around them, who held them, what was sung through them, or what was lost near them.

Objects should not be powerful because they are rare. They should be powerful because they are emotionally saturated.

### 4. Places Remember

The city stores emotional residue.

Locations can hold:

- grief
- celebration
- betrayal
- devotion
- violence
- protection
- longing
- ancestral rhythm

City memory should feel specific and lived-in, not abstract.

### 5. Music Can Shape Resonance

Music can:

- reveal
- soothe
- intensify
- protect
- call
- conceal
- distort
- synchronize
- fracture

Music should not solve every problem. It should create openings, consequences, and emotional exposure.

### 6. Resonance Has Cost

Interacting with resonance should have emotional or bodily weight.

Possible costs:

- exhaustion
- memory confusion
- emotional flooding
- unwanted inheritance
- relationship strain
- distorted perception
- becoming too visible to other resonant forces

### 7. Mystery Remains

The system should remain partly unknowable.

Creators can define rules for continuity, but the audience should experience resonance as emotional discovery, not as a textbook.
