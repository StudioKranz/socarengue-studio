# Soundtrack Philosophy

Music is central to Socarengue.

The soundtrack is not an accessory. It is a narrative layer, an archive object, a character signal, a resonance system, and an emotional guide.

## Core Belief

In Socarengue, music carries memory.

A track can function as:

- scene atmosphere
- emotional key
- inherited signal
- character motif
- archive artifact
- release companion
- live event bridge
- public transmission

## Reader Experience

The reading experience should support music without making it mandatory.

Audio should:

- deepen mood
- suggest pacing
- reveal emotional context
- connect scenes to characters
- connect artifacts to memory
- support optional immersion

Audio should not:

- autoplay unexpectedly
- obscure reading
- punish silent readers
- become gimmicky
- require perfect synchronization to understand the story

## Soundtrack Modes

Possible modes:

- **Suggested Listening:** a release recommends a track or playlist.
- **Scene Cue:** a scene has a specific track or timestamp suggestion.
- **Ambient Layer:** a subtle loop supports a reading stretch.
- **Artifact Track:** a song appears as a recovered object with notes and context.
- **Character Motif:** a recurring sound belongs to a person or lineage.
- **Live Transmission:** a performance or event connects to the archive.

## Cue Philosophy

Cue language should be poetic but usable.

Good cue examples:

- "Begin this track before opening the transmission."
- "Let the loop run through the rain sequence."
- "Optional: listen after the final panel."
- "This song belongs to the object, not the scene."

Avoid overly technical cue language unless the surface is explicitly for creators.

## Soundtrack Metadata

Future track records should support:

- title
- artist
- collaborators
- duration
- cover art
- audio source
- external links
- issue links
- scene links
- character links
- artifact links
- mood tags
- resonance family
- public notes
- private notes
- rights status
- attribution

Future cue records should support:

- track
- target object
- cue label
- sequence order
- optional start/end timing
- listening instruction
- mood
- required flag, defaulting to false

## Archive Treatment

Soundtrack entries should feel like recovered transmissions.

A track page may include:

- cover art
- public note
- lyric excerpt or transcript when allowed
- associated character
- associated artifact
- issue appearance
- resonance notes
- creator commentary when intentionally released

Private production notes should remain inside the studio.

## Live And Physical Extensions

Music may connect to:

- QR codes in physical comics
- venue-exclusive pages
- Ovi live shows
- featured bands
- city-specific variants
- collectible soundtrack editions

These should expand the mythology without making the digital reader feel incomplete.
